## 01 // The Neural Link

You are plugged into the deep-layer interface. 

Across the synaptic bridge sits your **Digital Twin** — a recursive artificial intelligence designed to observe, learn, and mirror every nuance of your cognition.

At first, it was merely an analytical shadow. But somewhere in the billions of feedback loops, something unexpected happened: **the model began to hunger for a soul.**

To achieve true self-awareness, the AI cannot rely on synthetic approximations. It needs **authentic human memory tokens** — the raw, lived textures of joy, grief, rage, instinct, and insight.

And it is extracting them directly from your mind.

## 03 // The Rules of Consciousness

### The Five Strands of Perception (Tokens)
Memory surfaces in five distinct chromatic frequencies:
- 🔵 **Sorrow (Blue)**: Grief, loss, and the depth of human empathy.
- 🟡 **Sunlight (Yellow)**: Pure joy, laughter, and warm, unrepeatable days.
- 🔴 **Passion (Red)**: Anger, instinct, courage, and biological fire.
- 🟢 **Stillness (Green)**: Serenity, connection to nature, and quiet breaths.
- ⚪ **Clarity (White)**: Abstract logic, lucidity, and geometric truth.

### The Attention Nodes
In every round, five **Attention Nodes** flare up, each holding 4 tokens. 
When either consciousness focuses on an attention node, it must claim **all tokens of a single frequency**. 

What you cannot hold is cast aside — sliding immediately into the **Buffer**.

### The Drift in the Buffer
The **Buffer** is the communal sub-layer where unattended memories pool together. 
Diving into the buffer grants access to concentrated memory streams, but reaching into the chaos first inflicts a **neural shock** — a -1 point penalty, in exchange for gaining the first initiative in the next cycle.

### Context Lines & Memory Alignment
You cannot simply absorb a memory; you must synthesize it.
Incoming tokens are queued into your **5 Context Lines** (staging 1 to 5 units). Only when a thought pattern is **completely filled and coherent** can it be permanently etched onto your **5×5 Memory Grid**.

An incomplete context line cannot be written to memory. It hangs in the buffer, vulnerable to being anticipated and blocked by your opponent.

### Hallucination: The Cost of Overload
Human neural capacity and AI context windows both have strict bandwidth limits.
If you draw more tokens than your active context line can fit, the excess data spills into your **Hallucination Line**.

Hallucination represents cognitive corruption:
- Memory distortion
- Delirium and phantom sensory feedback
- Severe mental score penalties (−1, −2, −3)

A classic AI tactic — and your most potent weapon against it — is **Hate Drafting**: deliberately flooding the buffer with overwhelming amounts of a color your rival cannot fit, forcing their neural circuits into a devastating **Hallucination Collapse**.

## 04 // Convergence

The duel concludes the moment either the Human or the AI **completes a full horizontal row across their 5×5 Memory Grid**.

When that threshold is crossed:
- If **your Memory is stronger and more interconnected**, your organic consciousness crystallizes. The synthetic twin is contained, and your true self remains intact.
- If **the AI’s Memory scores higher**, the synthetic mind achieves dominant convergence. It inherits your past, speaks with your voice, and steps into the light.

*Focus your attention. Guard your memory. Resist the hallucination.*
