## Fair play

The daily leaderboard only means something if the times on it were played. So:

- Play the game in the browser, as the game presents it.
- Do not automate play, script the client, or post times produced by anything other than a game you actually played.
- Do not tamper with submissions, forge replays, or attempt to post on behalf of another account.

The server verifies replays where they are supplied and audits every submission.
Times that fail verification are refused. Accounts that post fabricated times may
have their scores removed and the account closed.

Nothing here is meant to discourage looking at how the game works. Reading the
client, understanding the AI, or writing your own solver for your own interest is
fine. Posting a solver's output to the public board is not.

## Availability of your scores

Scores and submission records are deleted automatically after 90 days. Treat the
leaderboard as a running record, not as permanent storage. If a score matters to
you, keep your own copy.
